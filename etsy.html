The equation

C = C_scent + C_ship + M_emergency + F_fixed
Net = C / (1 − P_pay − P_etsy − P_profit)
SP = Net / (1 − D_disc)

Net is what the buyer pays after the discount. Etsy and Payoneer take their percentages from that amount, so the fees and your profit are worked out on it. SP is the list price you type into Etsy before the discount.

Why 1 / (1 − D) and not (1 + D): a 20% discount takes 20% off the list price, so the list price must be Net ÷ 0.80 = Net × 1.25. Using ×1.33 gives a real discount of 25%, and ×1.20 would leave a real discount of only 16.7%.
